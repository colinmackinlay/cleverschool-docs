---

Clever School Australia Document Management gives staff a single place to find the documents they need, without hunting through email threads or shared drives. You can organise your documents into clearly labelled categories, control who can see what, and keep on top of review dates — so your policies and procedures are always current and accessible.

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Structured Folders and Categories" icon="folder-open">
    Organise documents into groups — for example, policies, guidance, handbooks, and governor minutes — so staff can find what they need quickly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Permission-controlled Access" icon="lock">
    Restrict visibility of sensitive documents to authorised staff only. Everyone sees exactly what they need to, and nothing more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Version History" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Track changes over time so you can see what a document said previously and when it was updated.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review Date Reminders" icon="bell">
    Assign a document owner and set a review date. The owner receives a reminder as the review date approaches, so nothing gets overlooked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read Confirmations" icon="circle-check">
    Allocate documents to specific staff members and ask them to confirm they have read and understood the content. Managers can monitor responses and chase where needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Broad Document Support" icon="file-lines">
    Suitable for policies, procedures, governor minutes, staff handbooks, and any other reference documents your school needs to maintain.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Document Management is designed for internal staff use. It gives you a controlled, auditable record of what documents exist, who has access to them, and whether staff have read key materials.
</Note>
