Website Tools provides two practical utilities to enrich your school website with minimal setup. Whether you want to give parents a simple way to contact staff or add a weekly vocabulary feature to support pupil literacy, both tools are straightforward to configure and maintain.

## Staff Contact Forms

The Staff Contact Forms feature lets you create web-based contact forms for named members of staff or departments — ideal for a "Contact the Head of Year" form, a SENCO enquiry form, or a general admissions query page.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Named Staff or Departments" icon="address-card">
    Create forms for individual staff members or whole departments, and embed them directly on your school website.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Spam Protection" icon="robot">
    Built-in bot protection keeps your staff inboxes free from automated spam without inconveniencing genuine enquirers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Central Office Copy" icon="copy">
    Every incoming message is copied to an office address, so the school always has visibility of contact being made through the website.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quality-controlled Replies" icon="envelope-circle-check">
    Staff replies are routed via the office before being sent to the enquirer, ensuring consistency and quality in your school's communications.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Routing replies via the office means your school maintains oversight of all external communications sent through the contact form system — useful for safeguarding and communication standards.
</Note>

## Word of the Week

The Word of the Week feature provides the data behind a webpage that displays a different vocabulary word each week, complete with its meaning, an example sentence, and translations into one or two languages. It's a simple but effective way to support literacy across your school community.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Weekly Vocabulary" icon="spell-check">
    A new word appears each week, with its meaning, an example sentence, and one or two language translations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plan the Full Year in Advance" icon="calendar-days">
    Add your entire year's worth of words in one sitting. The system displays them in the right week automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Previous Words Archive" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Past words remain viewable on a separate archive page, so pupils can revisit vocabulary they've already encountered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Language Translations" icon="language">
    Include translations into one or two languages to support EAL pupils and reinforce vocabulary in context.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Setting up your Word of the Week entries for the whole academic year in one go at the start of term is the easiest way to keep the feature running without ongoing maintenance.
</Tip>
