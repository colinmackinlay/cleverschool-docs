This privacy policy explains how we use the personal data we collect from you when you use our websites.

### What data do we collect?

Cleverschool Australia collects the following data:

For employed staff:
- Name
- Work email address

For Sixth Form Applicants:
- Personal identification information (Name, email address, address, phone number, etc.)
- Predicted grades at your current school
- Initial course choices
- Any other data you choose to submit that you believe is relevant to your application

For Students Being Enrolled:
- Personal identification information (Name, email address, address, phone number, etc.)
- Optional additional information including:
- Contact details for your parents
- Information about dietary and medical needs as well as details of your GP

### How do we collect your data?

You directly provide Cleverschool Systems with most of the data we collect. We collect data and process data when you:

- Register online or place an order for any of our products or services.
- Voluntarily complete a survey or provide feedback on any of our web-forms or via email.
- Use or view our website via your browser’s cookies.
- How will we use your data?

Cleverschool Systems collects your data so that we can:

- Process your application or enrolment.
- Provide services for you to use in your school.
- Manage your subscription to our services.

Cleverschool Systems will not share your data with other organisations.

When Cleverschool Systems processes an order for services, it may send your data to, and also use the resulting information from, credit reference agencies to prevent fraudulent purchases.

### How do we store your data?
Cleverschool Systems securely stores your data on servers in the UK provided by DigitalOcean.

Cleverschool Systems will keep your data:

- For Staff, while employed in a school using our services and for any legally required period after you cease to be employed.
- For Sixth Form Applicants, until your application has been processed and you have either joined the school or withdrawn your application.
- For students being enrolled, until your enrolment has been processed and the data transferred to your school.
- Once this time period has expired, we will delete your data.

### Marketing
For students and parents, no marketing materials will be sent other than for the school you have applied to join.

For schools using our services we will send you updates about the products of ours that you use and information about products and services of ours that we think you might like.

If you have agreed to receive marketing, you may always opt out at a later date.

You have the right at any time to stop Cleverschool Systems from contacting you for marketing purposes.

If you no longer wish to be contacted for marketing purposes, please email support@cleverschool.au.

### What are your data protection rights?
Cleverschool Systems would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

- The right to access – You have the right to request Cleverschool Systems for copies of your personal data. We may charge you a small fee for this service.
- The right to rectification – You have the right to request that Cleverschool Systems correct any information you believe is inaccurate. You also have the right to request Cleverschool Systems to complete the information you believe is incomplete.
- The right to erasure – You have the right to request that Cleverschool Systems erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
- The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that Cleverschool Systems restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
- The right to object to processing – You have the right to object to Cleverschool Systems’ processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
- The right to data portability – You have the right to request that Cleverschool Systems transfer the data that we have collected to another organisation, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at our email: support@cleverschool.au

### Cookies
Cookies are text files placed on your computer to collect standard Internet log information and visitor behaviour information. When you visit our websites, we may collect information from you automatically through cookies or similar technology.

For further information, visit allaboutcookies.org.

### How do we use cookies?
Cleverschool Systems uses cookies in a range of ways to improve your experience on our website, including:

- Keeping you signed in
- Understanding how you use our website

### What types of cookies do we use?
There are a number of different types of cookies, however, our website uses:

- Functionality – Cleverschool Systems uses these cookies so that we recognise you on our website and remember your previously selected preferences. These could include what language you prefer and location you are in.

### How to manage cookies
You can set your browser not to accept cookies, and the above website tells you how to remove cookies from your browser. However, in a few cases, some of our website features may not function as a result.

### Privacy policies of other websites
The Cleverschool Systems website contains links to other websites. Our privacy policy applies only to our website, so if you click on a link to another website, you should read their privacy policy.

### Changes to our privacy policy
Cleverschool Systems keeps its privacy policy under regular review and places any updates on this web page. This privacy policy was last updated on 4 July 2022.

### How to contact us
If you have any questions about Cleverschool Systems privacy policy, the data we hold on you, or you would like to exercise one of your data protection rights, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Email us at: support@cleverschool.au

### How to contact the appropriate authority
Should you wish to report a complaint or if you feel that Cleverschool Systems has not addressed your concern in a satisfactory manner, you may contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.
