## Getting Started

Follow these four steps to set up Clever School Australia for your school.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register Your School">
    Go to [https://cleverschool.au/register](https://cleverschool.au/register) and fill in your school details to create your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Your Modules">
    From the Administration section, choose the modules your school needs and enable them one by one. Each module appears in the navigation as soon as it is switched on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Managers and Owners">
    For each enabled module, assign a Manager from your staff. Managers can then assign Owners to individual areas or items within their module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Using the Platform">
    Your team can now log in and begin working. Clever School Australia pre-loads sensible default content for each module so you can start straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Schools that are part of a group — such as a Multi-Academy Trust (MAT) or Local Authority (LA) — need to be set up centrally so that all schools are linked together correctly. Please contact [support@cleverschool.au](mailto:support@cleverschool.au) to arrange this before registering individually.
</Note>
