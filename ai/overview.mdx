The AI Tools module gives school staff a safe, school-managed way to explore AI tools without needing separate commercial accounts. Rather than asking staff to sign up for third-party services with personal email addresses, Clever School Systems provides AI capabilities within your existing school platform — keeping things simple, controlled, and appropriate for a school environment.

<Warning>
  AI Tools are currently experimental and in active development. Features may change as we iterate and improve them. We welcome your feedback.
</Warning>

## Current Tools

### AI Image Generation

Staff can use the AI image generation tool to create images for use in lessons, classroom displays, and presentations. This removes the need for a separate commercial AI account and keeps image generation within your school's managed environment.

<Note>
  Currently, only the AI image generation tool is enabled. Additional tools are in development and will be made available as they are ready.
</Note>

## Why Use School-Managed AI?

Using AI through Clever School Systems rather than through personal commercial accounts has some important advantages for schools.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="No Personal Accounts Needed" icon="user-slash">
    Staff can explore AI capabilities without signing up for external services using personal or school email addresses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Controlled Environment" icon="shield-halved">
    All AI usage takes place within your school's managed platform, not on external commercial services outside your oversight.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Stays Within Managed Systems" icon="server">
    You retain visibility of how AI tools are being used, without data flowing through unmanaged third-party accounts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Appropriate for Schools" icon="school">
    Tools are provided in a context that's designed for school use, rather than general commercial or consumer AI products.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
