There are lots of features in each of the modules and the best way to discover those is to try them out.
Below you will find suggestions for things you can try in key modules.
They are grouped into three levels of challenge. Please do get in touch if you have any queries or suggestions!

    <AccordionGroup>
        <Accordion title="101" icon="person">
            - Approve one of the risk assessments
            - Confirm that you have read an approved assessment
            - View who else has confirmed the risk assessment
            - Print out the risk assessment
        </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="Developing" icon="person-walking">
            - Add a risk assessment for a child boiling a kettle!
            - Add a "Scalds" hazard to it with high likelihood
            - Add some control measures
            - Revise the likelihood and/or severity of the hazard
        </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="Extending" icon="person-running">
            - Add a dated action for someone to a risk assessment for next week
            - Re-order the risk/hazards in one of the risk assessments
            - Email the owner or approver of the risk assessment
            - Write a "proper" risk assessment for a school activity
        </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
</Card>

<Card title="Ofsted Self Evaluation" icon="lightbulb">
    <AccordionGroup>

            - Print out the Quality of Education section
        </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="Developing" icon="person-walking">

            - Change the owner of a section
            - Email the owner of a section with some feedback
        </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="Extending" icon="person-running">
            - Use "Manage" to archive or restore the Early Years section
            - Print the whole report with everything, then omitting some details
            - Use "Manage" to add a new item to an area or re-order the items
            - Use "Manage" to create a new SEF area
        </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
</Card>

<Card title="Safety Audit" icon="user-hard-hat">
    <AccordionGroup>
        <Accordion title="101" icon="person">
            - CLick on the "General" area to view the status of its items
            - Edit some of the items and un-tick them - add comments
            - Re-confirm them as checked - note how the due date and status update
            - Print out the section
        </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="Developing" icon="person-walking">
            - Use the search box to narrow down the areas on display
            - Change the number of items per page to see all of the areas
            - Change the owner of some areas and cascade to the items for some
            - Filter the list by area owner
        </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="Extending" icon="person-running">
            - Use "Review Safety Items" to see all items and their status
            - Filter the list by status
            - Search the list with the search box
            - Email one of the owners about the item
        </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
</Card>
