User management in Clever School Australia lets you control who can access each module and what they can do within it. Every staff member who needs to use any part of the platform requires a user account, and you control their access precisely by assigning roles on a module-by-module basis.

## Adding Users

You can invite any staff member to Clever School Australia by adding them from the Administration section. They'll receive an email invitation and set their own password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Administration > Users">
    Open the Administration section from the left navigation sidebar, then select **Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add User">
    Click the **Add User** button at the top of the Users list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the staff member's details">
    Enter the staff member's full name and their school email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invitation sent">
    Clever School Australia sends them an email invitation. They follow the link to set their password and verify their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Users must verify their email address before they can log in for the first time. If a staff member hasn't received their invitation, see the [Unverified](#unverified) section below.
</Note>

## User Roles

Each module in Clever School Australia has its own set of roles. Assigning a role to a user for a given module grants them access to that module and defines what they can do within it. A user with no module roles assigned cannot access any module content.

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Full access to all settings. Can enable modules and assign all roles across the platform. |
| Module Manager | Manages a specific module. Can assign Owners within that module. |
| Module Owner | Responsible for areas or items within a module (for example, a Compliance Owner for a compliance category). |
| Staff / User | Can read and contribute to the modules they have been given access to. |

## Managing User Statuses

Every user account in Clever School Australia has a status that reflects their current relationship with your school. You can change a user's status at any time from **Administration > Users**.

### Unverified

The user has been invited but hasn't yet clicked the link in their invitation email and confirmed their email address. They cannot log in until they verify.

To resend the invitation, go to **Users > Unverified**, find the staff member, and select **Resend Invitation**.

### Active

The normal working state. An active user can log in and access any module they have a role for.

### Archived

The user has left the school. Their account data and contributions are fully retained, but they cannot log in. Use **Archive** whenever a staff member leaves your school — this keeps your historical records intact while preventing access.

### Blocked

Access has been suspended. The user cannot log in until an Administrator unblocks them. Use this status when you need to suspend access temporarily without archiving the account.

## Wonde Integration

If your school uses Wonde for MIS integration, you can import and sync staff accounts automatically rather than adding them one by one. Wonde keeps your user list in step with your MIS, so new starters and leavers are reflected without manual intervention.

To set up Wonde integration for your school, contact the Clever School Australia support team at [support@cleverschool.au](mailto:support@cleverschool.au).

## External Authentication (SSO)

Clever School Australia supports single sign-on (SSO) via **Google** and **Microsoft**. When SSO is enabled, your staff log in using their existing school credentials instead of a separate Clever School Australia password.

To enable SSO, go to **Administration > Authentication**, choose your provider (Google or Microsoft), and follow the on-screen configuration steps. Once configured, your staff will see the option to sign in with their school account on the login page.

<Tip>
  Enabling SSO means your staff don't need to remember a separate password for Clever School Australia. It also means that when a staff member's school account is disabled (for example, when they leave), their Clever School Australia access is automatically revoked at the same time.
</Tip>
