The Administration section is the control centre for your entire Clever School Systems platform. From here you configure every aspect of how the platform works for your school — from which modules are active, to who can access them, and how your staff sign in. Only users who have been granted the **Administrator** role can access this section.

## What You Can Do

The Administration section gives you full control over your school's Clever School Systems setup. As an Administrator, you can:

- **Enable and disable modules** — activate only the modules your school needs, and turn them off at any time without losing data
- **Add and manage staff accounts** — invite staff by email and manage their accounts throughout their time at your school
- **Assign roles and permissions per module** — control what each staff member can see and do within each individual module
- **Configure external authentication (SSO)** — allow staff to sign in with their existing Google or Microsoft school credentials
- **Manage user statuses** — keep your user list accurate by archiving leavers and blocking access when needed
- **Set up group-level controls** — for schools operating within a MAT or LA, certain settings can be managed centrally across all schools in your group

## Accessing Administration

From any page in Clever School Systems, click **Administration** in the left navigation sidebar to open the Administration section.

If you don't see **Administration** in the sidebar, you don't currently have the Administrator role. Ask your school's Administrator to grant you the Administrator role before continuing.

<Note>
  In multi-school groups (MATs and LAs), some Administration settings can be managed centrally by the group administrator, so individual schools don't need to configure them separately. If certain settings appear read-only or pre-configured, they may have been set at group level.
</Note>
