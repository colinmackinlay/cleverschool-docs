The Enrolment module streamlines new pupil intake by letting parents submit all required information online, eliminating paper forms and manual data entry. Parents log in at a time that suits them, complete the form at their own pace, and receive a copy of everything they submitted. Your school gets accurate, structured data that imports directly into your Management Information System (MIS).

## Benefits for Your School

- **Streamlined process** — replace paper packs with a single, guided online form.
- **Saves time entering data into your MIS** — export an ATF file and import it in minutes rather than entering records manually.
- **Eliminates data entry errors** — data comes straight from parents, reducing transcription mistakes.
- **Collects medical and educational information** — capture everything you need before the first day.
- **Gathers key consents** — collect consent for use of images, local trips, and biometric data in one place.
- **File record for school and parents** — both parties have a record of exactly what was submitted.

## Benefits for Parents

- **Easy login** — parents can sign in using a social media account or a traditional email and password.
- **Efficient information transfer** — a single, structured form replaces multiple paper documents sent back and forth.
- **Space for detailed information** — parents can provide thorough personal information about their child, including medical needs and other important context.
- **Confirmation of receipt** — parents receive a record of what the school has received, giving them reassurance.

## MIS Integration

Once parents have submitted their enrolment forms, you can export an ATF (Admissions Transfer File) directly from the Enrolment module. The ATF is the DfE-standard format used by leading MIS providers, so you can import it straight into your MIS — whether that's SIMS, Bromcom, Arbor, or another compatible system.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite parents">
    Add your new intake students to the Enrolment module. Parents receive an email invitation with a secure login link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Parents complete the form">
    Parents log in and complete the enrolment form, providing contact details, medical information, and consents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the ATF file">
    Once submissions are in, export the ATF file from the Enrolment module dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import into your MIS">
    Import the ATF file into your MIS. Pupil records are created automatically — no manual data entry required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Medical information and consent records collected during enrolment are stored separately from the ATF export and can be printed per student for your school's physical or digital filing system.
</Note>
