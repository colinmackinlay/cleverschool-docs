This guide takes you through setting up your school calendar, adding events, and publishing iCal links so that staff and parents can subscribe on their personal devices. Once published, your calendar updates automatically across every device that has subscribed — no manual re-sending required.

<Note>
  Initial setup requires the **Administrator** role to enable the module and assign a Calendar Manager. Adding events and managing calendars is then handled by the Calendar Manager.
</Note>

## Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Calendar module">
    Go to **Administration > Modules** and enable the **Calendar** module.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign a Calendar Manager">
    Go to **Administration > Users** and assign the **Calendar Manager** role to the appropriate member of staff. The Calendar Manager is responsible for creating calendars, adding events, and managing published iCal links.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Calendar">
    Select **Calendar** from the left-hand navigation menu to access your calendar management area.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Calendars">
    Create one or more **Calendars** tailored to different audiences. For example:

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Staff Dates" icon="chalkboard-teacher">
        Internal dates, meetings, INSET days, and deadlines for staff.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Parents / Carers" icon="family">
        Term dates, school events, trips, and parent evenings.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Governors" icon="landmark">
        Governor meeting dates, key reporting deadlines, and inspection-related events.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    Having separate calendars means each audience only subscribes to the events that are relevant to them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Sub-sections">
    Within each calendar, create **Sub-sections** to group related events together. For example, within the Parents/Carers calendar you might have sub-sections for Term Dates, Trips, and Parents' Evenings. Sub-sections make it easier to manage and navigate events as the calendar grows.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add events">
    Add events to the appropriate sub-section. For each event, provide:
    - **Title** — a clear, descriptive name
    - **Date and time** — including end time where relevant
    - **Description** — any additional detail parents or staff need to know
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish the calendar">
    Once you are ready, **publish the calendar** to generate its unique iCal URL. This URL is what you share with staff, parents, and governors so they can subscribe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing via iCal

Each published calendar generates a **unique iCal URL**. Share this URL via your school newsletter, website, or email communication. Recipients paste the URL into their calendar app to subscribe — the calendar then updates automatically on their device whenever you add or change an event.

iCal subscriptions work with all major calendar applications:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="google">
    Use "Add calendar > From URL" in Google Calendar settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apple Calendar" icon="apple">
    Use "File > New Calendar Subscription" in Apple Calendar or iPhone Settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Microsoft Outlook" icon="microsoft">
    Use "Add calendar > Subscribe from web" in Outlook or Outlook.com.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Unlike a one-off export, an iCal subscription is a live link. When you update, add, or remove an event in Clever School Australia, the change is automatically reflected in every subscribed calendar — you do not need to re-share anything.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Staff can also sign up to receive a **weekly email digest** of upcoming calendar events, giving them a summary of what is happening in the week ahead without needing to check the calendar directly.
</Tip>

## Rolling Forward to the Next Year

At the start of each new academic year, use the **Roll Forward** function to copy your existing events into the new year. Clever School Australia advances the dates automatically to match the new academic calendar, saving you from recreating recurring events like term dates, INSET days, and regular meetings from scratch.

<Tip>
  Run Roll Forward after you have confirmed the new year's term dates. Review the copied events afterwards to check for any dates that fall on different days of the week or need adjusting for the new year.
</Tip>
